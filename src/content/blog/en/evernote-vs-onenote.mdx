Evernote and OneNote are two of the oldest and most established note-taking applications. While newer tools have gained popularity, both veterans continue to evolve and serve millions of users. Let us examine how they stack up today.

## Evernote Overview

Evernote pioneered the digital note-taking space and remains a powerful option. Its web clipper is still best-in-class for saving web content. The search functionality is exceptional, capable of finding text within images and PDFs. Recent updates have added AI-powered features for note summarization and cleanup.

## OneNote Overview

OneNote offers a unique freeform canvas that mimics a physical notebook. You can place text, images, drawings, and files anywhere on the page. Deep integration with Microsoft 365 makes it natural for users in the Microsoft ecosystem. The hierarchical organization of Notebooks, Sections, and Pages is intuitive.

## Feature Comparison

Evernote excels at web clipping, search, and tagging. OneNote excels at freeform note-taking, drawing, and Microsoft integration. Evernote's interface is more structured, while OneNote offers more creative freedom. Both support collaboration, though OneNote's real-time co-authoring is smoother.

## Pricing

OneNote is completely free with a Microsoft account. Evernote's free tier is very limited (50 notes), with paid plans starting at $14.99 per month. This significant price difference makes OneNote the clear value winner.

## Verdict

Choose Evernote if web clipping and powerful search are priorities and you are willing to pay. Choose OneNote if you want a free, flexible tool with excellent Microsoft integration and drawing capabilities.

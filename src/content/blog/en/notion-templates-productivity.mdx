Notion's flexibility is both its greatest strength and biggest challenge. With infinite customization possibilities, knowing where to start can be overwhelming. These curated templates provide proven starting points for common use cases.

## Personal Dashboard

A personal dashboard template combines your daily tasks, calendar, notes, and goals into a single view. The best dashboards use linked databases to pull information from different areas of your workspace. Include a daily journal section, habit tracker, and reading list for a comprehensive personal system.

## Project Management

Project management templates should include a project database with status tracking, a tasks database linked to projects, and timeline views for deadline management. Add a meeting notes section linked to projects for complete context. The key is creating views that show the right information at the right time.

## Knowledge Base

A knowledge base template organizes your team's documentation into searchable categories. Include sections for company policies, standard operating procedures, onboarding guides, and technical documentation. Use tags and linked databases to create connections between related documents.

## Content Calendar

Content calendar templates help marketing teams plan, create, and publish content across channels. Include fields for content status, publish date, channel, author, and approval workflow. Calendar and kanban views help visualize the publishing schedule.

## CRM and Sales Pipeline

A simple CRM template tracks contacts, companies, deals, and interactions. Link contacts to companies and deals to contacts for complete relationship visibility. Pipeline views show deal stages, while calendar views track follow-up dates.

## Where to Find Templates

Notion's official template gallery, community forums, and third-party marketplaces offer thousands of templates. Start with official templates for reliability, then customize them to fit your specific needs. Remember that the best template is one you actually use consistently.

Open source software has matured to the point where it can compete with commercial alternatives in almost every category. These tools offer not just cost savings but also privacy, customization, and community-driven development.

## Office Suites

LibreOffice provides a complete office suite with word processing, spreadsheets, presentations, and more. OnlyOffice offers excellent Microsoft Office compatibility with a modern interface. Both are actively maintained and suitable for professional use.

## Note-Taking

Joplin is an open source alternative to Evernote with end-to-end encryption and sync options. Logseq combines outlining with knowledge graph features for a powerful thinking tool. Both store data in standard formats that you fully control.

## Project Management

OpenProject and Taiga provide robust project management with features comparable to commercial tools. They support agile methodologies, Gantt charts, and team collaboration. Self-hosting gives you complete control over your project data.

## Communication

Mattermost is a self-hosted Slack alternative with channels, direct messaging, and integrations. Jitsi Meet provides video conferencing without account requirements or time limits. Both can be deployed on your own infrastructure for maximum privacy.

## File Management

Nextcloud offers a self-hosted alternative to Google Workspace, including file storage, calendar, contacts, and collaborative document editing. With numerous plugins, you can build a comprehensive productivity suite that you fully control.

## Getting Started

Most open source tools offer hosted versions for easy evaluation before self-hosting. Start with one or two tools that address your biggest pain points. The open source community provides extensive documentation and support for getting started.
